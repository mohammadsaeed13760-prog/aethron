01 — About

The organization behind our intelligent products.

AETHRON is an independent technology, artificial intelligence and innovation organization. We develop intelligent systems, software applications, digital platforms and advanced technological solutions.

As the parent organization, AETHRON brings its AI products, research initiatives, applications and digital projects together under one identity, with one standard for how they are built.

Above all, what we build should be useful: tools that help people learn faster, work better and get more done every day.
